Help & Support

Learn how to make the most out of the report generator.

Navigate to the Report Builder tab. Fill out the required fields such as your project title, team members, and abstract. You can use the AI Assistant to help draft sections. Once complete, click "Generate Report" to preview and download your formatted file.

The AI Assistant is available both in the Report Builder and as a standalone chat tool. It can help you expand bullet points, fix grammar, or structure your methodology section. Just select your project context from the dropdown in the AI Assistant tab so it knows what you are working on.

Yes! If you download your report as a DOCX file, it is fully editable in Microsoft Word or Google Docs. The PDF version is locked and ideal for final submission.
